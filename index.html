Our services

Book lending

Borrow up to 8 books for 21 days. Renew online or at the desk.

Study rooms

Quiet rooms for one to six people. Book a two-hour slot at the front desk.

Computers and Wi-Fi

20 computers, free Wi-Fi and printing at low cost for all members.

Children’s corner

Picture books, story time and a safe space for readers under 12.

Research help

Our librarians help you find sources, cite them and use databases.

Digital library

Read e-books and listen to audiobooks from home with your member card.
